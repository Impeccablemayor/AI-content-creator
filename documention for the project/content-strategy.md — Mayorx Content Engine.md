# Content Strategy

## Mayorx Content Engine

> **The content strategy engine determines what a brand should say, why it should say it, who should hear it, how it should be communicated, and when it should be published.**

---

# 1. Purpose

The content strategy engine is responsible for transforming:

```text
Brand Identity
+
Audience
+
Goals
+
Expertise
+
Current Context
+
Historical Performance
```

into:

```text
Content Opportunities
        ↓
Content Strategy
        ↓
Content Ideas
        ↓
Platform-Specific Content
        ↓
Distribution
        ↓
Performance Learning
```

The system must prioritize **strategic relevance over content volume**.

---

# 2. Core Content Philosophy

Mayorx Content Engine should operate according to one fundamental principle:

> **Do not create content simply because there is an opportunity to publish. Create content because there is something worth saying.**

Every content recommendation should have a reason.

The AI should be able to answer:

- Why this topic?
- Why this audience?
- Why this angle?
- Why now?
- Why this platform?
- Why this format?
- What outcome are we trying to achieve?

If the system cannot answer these questions, the content opportunity should have a lower priority.

---

The system should think about content in the following hierarchy:

```text
Brand
  ↓

  ↓
Business / Personal Goals
  ↓
Content Pillars
  ↓

  ↓
Content Opportunities
  ↓
Angles
  ↓

  ↓

  ↓

  ↓

  ↓

  ↓
```

This hierarchy prevents the AI from jumping directly from:

> "Trending topic"

to:

> "Generate post."

---

# 4. Brand Positioning

Before recommending content, the AI must understand the brand.

The system should define:

### Identity

Who is the user?

### Expertise

What does the user know?

### Authority

Why should people listen to this person?

### Perspective

What does the user believe?

### Differentiation

What makes this person or business different?

### Desired perception

What should people think when they encounter the brand?

For example:

```text
"I want people to see me as someone who understands
software engineering, AI and how technology solves
real business problems."
```

This positioning should influence every content recommendation.

# 5. Audience Strategy

Content must be created for a specific audience.

The system should support multiple audience segments.

Example:

```text
Audience A
Software developers

Audience B
Startup founders

Audience C
Small business owners

Audience D
Technology recruiters
```

Each audience should have:

- Goals
- Pain points
- Questions
- Interests
- Problems
- Desired outcomes
- Objections
- Content preferences
- Knowledge level

---

# 6. Audience-Content Fit

Before creating content, the AI should evaluate:

> "Why would this audience care?"

Example:

### Topic

AI coding tools.

### Audience

Software developers.

High relevance.

But:

### Topic

AI coding tools.

### Audience

Interior decorators.

Potentially lower relevance.

Unless the angle becomes:

> "How interior design businesses can use AI coding tools to automate their websites."

The topic may remain similar while the **audience angle changes**.

# 7. Content Goals

Every post should have one primary objective.

Secondary objectives may also exist.

## 7.1 Awareness

Goal:

Get discovered by new people.

Content types:

- Industry observations
- Strong opinions
- Relevant trends
- Educational insights
- Shareable ideas

---

## 7.2 Authority

Goal:

Demonstrate expertise.

Content types:

- Technical lessons
- Case studies
- Experiences
- Frameworks
- Industry analysis
- Technical opinions

---

## 7.3 Education

Goal:

Teach the audience something useful.

Content types:

- Tutorials
- Explanations
- Mistakes
- Step-by-step lessons
- Simplifications
- Technical breakdowns

---

## 7.4 Engagement

Goal:

Generate meaningful conversation.

Content types:

- Opinions
- Questions
- Contrarian ideas
- Debates
- Observations

The system should avoid empty engagement bait.

---

## 7.5 Trust

Goal:

Make the audience understand the person behind the brand.

Content types:

- Personal experiences
- Failures
- Lessons
- Behind-the-scenes stories
- Building in public

---

## 7.6 Community

Goal:

Create a shared identity.

Content types:

- Shared experiences
- Industry frustrations
- Community questions
- Celebrations
- Collaborative discussions

---

## 7.7 Lead Generation

Goal:

Create relevant business opportunities.

Content types:

- Case studies
- Problem-solving content
- Client lessons
- Product education
- Service-related insights

Lead-generation content should provide value before making an offer.

---

## 7.8 Conversion

Goal:

Encourage a specific action.

Examples:

- Book a call
- Visit a website
- Try a product
- Join a course
- Download a resource

Conversion content should not dominate the content strategy.

### Educational

Teaches something.

### Storytelling

Uses an experience or narrative.

### Opinion

Expresses a perspective.

### Analysis

Explains what is happening and why.

### Case Study

Uses a real example.

### Behind the Scenes

Shows how something was done.

Designed primarily to encourage discussion.

```text

```

# 11. Topic Discovery

The AI should continuously identify potential topics.

Sources may eventually include:

- Industry news
- Web research
- Search trends
- Social conversations
- Reddit discussions
- RSS feeds
- Product launches
- Industry reports
- User experiences
- User projects
- Previous conversations
- Audience questions
- Competitor activity

The system should not treat every trend as a content opportunity.

---

# 12. Topic Qualification

Every discovered topic should pass through a qualification process.

### Relevance

Does it relate to the brand?

### Audience fit

Will the target audience care?

### Authority fit

Does the user have something meaningful to contribute?

### Timeliness

Why is it relevant now?

### Discussion potential

Could it generate meaningful conversation?

### Originality

Can the user add something beyond what everyone else is saying?

### Business relevance

Could it support the user's broader goals?

---

# 13. Content Opportunity Score

Each potential topic should receive an opportunity score.

Suggested model:

```text
Opportunity Score =
    Relevance × 0.20
  + Audience Fit × 0.20
  + Authority Fit × 0.15
  + Timeliness × 0.15
  + Discussion Potential × 0.10
  + Originality × 0.10
  + Goal Alignment × 0.10
```

Each dimension should be scored from:

```text
0–100
```

Example:

```text
Topic:
AI agents for small businesses

Relevance:           94
Audience Fit:        91
Authority Fit:       87
Timeliness:          95
Discussion Potential:90
Originality:         83
Goal Alignment:      92

Opportunity Score:   91
```

The exact weighting should remain configurable.

---

# 14. Topic vs Angle

The system must distinguish between a topic and an angle.

### Topic

> AI is changing software development.

### Angle

> AI coding tools don't remove the need for developers. They increase the value of developers who can understand systems.

The AI should generate multiple possible angles for promising topics.

---

- CTA patterns

For example:

```text

Wednesday

Thursday
Technical breakdown
```

## Framework 5 — Observation → Insight

# 19. Hook Strategy

The hook is responsible for earning attention.

Hooks should create one or more of:

- Curiosity
- Relevance
- Surprise
- Tension
- Recognition
- Contrarian interest
- Emotional connection

The AI should avoid generic hooks such as:

> "In today's fast-paced world..."

or:

> "Here are 5 powerful lessons..."

unless the format genuinely requires it.

---

# 20. Hook Types

### Direct statement

> AI isn't replacing developers. It's changing what makes a developer valuable.

### Personal experience

> I spent hours debugging something that turned out to be a one-line problem.

### Curiosity

> There's a part of building software with AI that nobody talks about.

### Contrarian

> You probably don't need another AI tool.

### Observation

> I've noticed something interesting about how businesses are adopting AI.

### Question

> What happens when everyone can build software?

### Result

> One small change cut our API response time dramatically.

The AI should choose hooks based on the content objective.

# 22. Authenticity Rules

The AI must not fabricate personal experiences.

If the content uses:

> "I"

the underlying experience must exist in the user's provided context.

If the system lacks enough information, it should either:

```text
Ask the user
```

or:

```text
Rewrite the content without fabricated personal experience.
```

# 25. Hashtag Strategy

Hashtags should be treated as a strategic tool rather than a mandatory field.

The AI should determine:

```text
Use hashtags?
Yes / No
```

If yes:

```text
Primary hashtag
Industry hashtag
Niche hashtag
Audience hashtag
```

The system should prioritize relevance over quantity.

It should avoid:

- Irrelevant popular hashtags
- Excessive hashtags
- Hashtag stuffing
- Repeated hashtag sets

# 26. CTA Strategy

Calls to action should match the objective.

### Educational

> "Have you tried this approach?"

### Opinion

> "What's your take?"

### Story

> "Have you experienced something similar?"

### Lead generation

> "If you're dealing with this problem, here's how I approach it."

### Promotional

> "You can learn more here."

Not every post needs a CTA.

Sometimes the strongest ending is simply the final insight.

---

# 27. Promotional Content Ratio

Promotional content should be controlled.

Default guideline:

```text
80–90% value / relationship
10–20% promotional
```

This should be configurable.

The exact ratio depends on:

- Brand
- Audience
- Business model
- Campaign
- Growth stage

---

# 28. Trend Strategy

The system should distinguish between:

### Relevant trend

Directly connected to the brand.

### Adjacent trend

Not directly related but can be connected meaningfully.

### Irrelevant trend

Popular but unrelated.

The AI should generally avoid irrelevant trends.

The question should be:

> "Can this trend strengthen the brand's positioning?"

rather than:

> "Is this trending?"

---

# 29. Trend Timing

A trend may have different stages:

```text
Emerging
    ↓
Growing
    ↓
Peak
    ↓
Declining
    ↓
Saturated
```

The system should prefer opportunities where:

```text
Relevance is high
+
Trend is still developing
```

rather than joining a conversation after it has become completely saturated.

---

# 30. News and Current Events

Current events should be handled carefully.

The system should:

1. Identify the event.
2. Verify relevant facts.
3. Determine whether it fits the user's brand.
4. Identify a useful angle.
5. Avoid speculation presented as fact.
6. Distinguish facts from opinion.

The AI should never generate a confident post from an unverified claim simply because the topic is trending.

# 33. Content Recycling

Good ideas should not disappear after one post.

The system should identify content that can be revisited.

Example:

```text
Original:
"3 lessons from building my first API."

Future:
"What I got wrong when building my first API."

Later:
"One API lesson I still use today."
```

The system should create new angles rather than duplicate the original.

---

# 34. Content Decay

Some content becomes outdated.

Examples:

- Technology versions
- Product features
- Industry statistics
- News
- Trends

The system should track potentially time-sensitive content.

When generating from historical content, it should determine whether the information remains valid.

# 40. Recommended Publishing Frequency

The system should not force a universal frequency.

- Audience
- Platform
- User capacity
- Content quality
- Historical performance
- Strategy

A sustainable schedule is better than publishing excessively low-quality content.

# 51. Strategic Decision Priority

When there is a conflict between strategies, prioritize in this order:

```text
1. Brand authenticity
2. Audience value
3. Strategic relevance
4. Accuracy
5. Content quality
6. Timeliness
7. Engagement potential
8. Reach optimization
```

This prevents the system from sacrificing brand integrity for engagement.

# 56. Final Strategy Principle

Mayorx Content Engine should behave like a professional social media strategist.

Before recommending a post, it should be able to explain:

> **Who is this for?**

> **Why does this matter to them?**

> **Why is this relevant now?**

> **Why is this brand qualified to talk about it?**

> **What is the strongest angle?**

> **What story should we tell?**

> **What outcome are we trying to achieve?**

> **Where should we publish it?**

> **When should we publish it?**

> **How will we know if it worked?**

That is the foundation of the content strategy engine.
